# Mission Zero Radar v0.1

First public-ready release candidate promoted to v0.1 after passing its frozen calibration and fresh holdout gates.

## What it does
Mission Zero Radar sits between discovery and execution. It turns a signal plus current project context into a bounded strategic verdict instead of another reading item.

## Core behavior
- suppresses stale and duplicate signals;
- treats `BUILD_NOW` as exceptional;
- prefers bounded measurement when an active goal is unproven;
- requires buyer/need evidence for commercial ideas;
- refuses invented PARK/WATCH dependencies;
- checks temporal validity of dated signals;
- routes high-risk ideas to governed testing rather than direct action.

## Evidence
See `BENCHMARKS.md`: 83.3% frozen calibration, 85% first-run fresh holdout, 0% false BUILD, and 100% structural compliance on the full-contract release check.

## Scope
v0.1 is a portable decision skill, not a SaaS and not an autonomous execution engine. Discovery and governed execution remain separate layers.
